# Collateral Appraisal System

A bank's collateral appraisal workflow: requests come in, appraisals are performed and checked, and committees approve
the appraised values.

## Language

### Committee approval

**Conflicted member**:
A committee member who took part in an earlier step of the same appraisal (as presenter, book verifier, section
manager or assistant director), and therefore may not vote on its approval.
_Avoid_: Sign-off staff, interested member
